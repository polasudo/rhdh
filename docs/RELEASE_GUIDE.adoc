## During GA week

. Push errata(s) to stage for QE review
. Remind docs they must review/approve the errata
. Collect GO/NO-GO votes from stakeholders
..  _TODO_: Collect list of owners from various stakeholders (Linda's Go/No-Go list)

. Remind QE to move the errata to REL_PREP **ONLY WHEN ALL TESTS ARE DONE**
.. GA push will go out automatically via Release Driver

## On GA Day

. link:https://gitlab.cee.redhat.com/rhidp/rhdh/-/blob/rhdh-1-rhel-9/build/helm/prepare.sh[Push helm chart release]
.. Verify install works via clusterbot smoke test
. Monitor container image release(s) via errata(s)

### Once errata(s) are live

. Smoke test the ability to install from `stable` and upgrade to `stable-1.1` operator subscription channel

. Review websites
.. Developers.redhat.com
.. Access.redhat.com (docs, other)
.. Customer portal

. Check in with marketing re:
.. Internal announcement? External announcement? Other marketing / blogs / videos? Summit alignment / presentations?

## Other considerations

Ongoing through the quarter, remember to:

. Monitor GH-GL syncs and pipelines, as things can fail unexpectedly in OSBS or on gitlab runners
. Monitor quay image updates to ensure everything is kept current and fresh relative to upstram branches
